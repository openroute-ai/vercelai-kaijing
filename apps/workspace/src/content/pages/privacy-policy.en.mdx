Kaijing (开镜, website [vercelai.cn](https://vercelai.cn)) is an open-source short-drama and generation workspace. This Privacy Policy explains how the official distribution stores information locally and when information may leave your environment.

Parts of the workspace UI originated in the BeatDesign open-source project. Source attribution and licenses are in LICENSE, NOTICE, and related notices in the repository. That does not change Kaijing as the public product name.

## 1. Local-first by default

Projects and workspace state are stored in the operator-configured database. A default local install uses a SQLite file on this computer. Projects stay on this computer by default; you can export a project package and continue work elsewhere.

Opening, editing, arranging, importing, or exporting a project does not send project contents to the project maintainers. Local files are kept in the local project space until you confirm generation or another action that must transmit data.

## 2. Information stored in the workspace

Depending on the features you use, Kaijing may store locally or in an operator-controlled environment:

- Project names, scripts and boards, aspect ratio, canvas documents, studio settings, node positions, and workflow state.
- Imported images, video, audio, and local asset indexes.
- Prompts, generation parameters, model choices, job identifiers, status history, and output URLs.
- Your own keys saved in advanced settings (encrypted), redacted credential previews, and storage settings.
- Technical records needed to restore projects, prevent duplicate submissions, and show generation history.

The operator of the install controls the corresponding database and associated files.

## 3. Accounts, sign-in, and credits

If you choose to sign in, the operator stores the account information needed for registration, sessions, and credit ledgers. New users receive a starter credit amount intended to cover a first episode or first shot (the amount shown on the account page is authoritative; it may currently be a clearly labeled placeholder). Credit balances, grants, and deductions are kept in the operator-controlled ledger.

Browsing the interface and writing a script do not require sign-in. Confirming generation, using starter credits, or starting a top-up requires sign-in and may process account and billing information.

## 4. Information sent after you confirm generation

When you confirm generation, Kaijing sends the information required to complete the request. Depending on the workflow, this may include prompts and parameters, selected reference media, session credentials or your own key from advanced settings, and identifiers needed for status, errors, and outputs.

By default these requests go through the Kaijing platform gateway and may be processed by upstream AI, infrastructure, storage, and security providers. If you use your own key in advanced settings, that provider’s privacy policy also applies.

## 5. API keys and storage credentials

The ordinary path uses the platform gateway and does not ask you to enter a third-party key. If you save your own key or storage credentials in advanced settings, Kaijing encrypts them before writing them to the local or operator database.

Protect the devices, servers, environment files, databases, encryption keys, backups, and admin access associated with your install.

## 6. Storage and generated media

In the default SQLite environment, imported local assets are copied into a project-specific local directory. When a confirmed workflow needs remote reference media, Kaijing may upload it to platform storage or an operator-configured bucket.

Generated files may remain at URLs returned by the platform or upstream providers. Download important outputs, or export a project package for locally stored content, if you need a long-term copy.

## 7. Browser data, cookies, and telemetry

Kaijing may store language, theme, and one-time preferences such as “just look around” in the browser or necessary cookies. The open-source distribution does not include advertising trackers or maintainer-operated behavioral analytics, and does not send product telemetry to maintainers by default.

## 8. Self-hosting and third-party deployments

If you use a deployment run by someone else, that operator may change the software, connect extra services, enable logs or analytics, require accounts, or set their own retention rules. They should disclose those practices and handle privacy requests for that deployment.

## 9. Use and disclosure

The open-source project does not receive your local workspace data by default. Information you send to the platform gateway or a third-party generation service is used to complete the requested workflow, validate requests, operate credits and billing, protect the service, troubleshoot, comply with law, and use necessary vendors.

## 10. Retention and deletion

Local project data remains under your or the operator’s control until deleted according to that environment’s backup and retention rules. Deleting a local project may not delete information already sent to the platform, storage providers, or upstream models. For access, correction, export, or deletion of cloud data held by the product operator, use the contact details published on [vercelai.cn](https://vercelai.cn).

## 11. Security

Kaijing uses reasonable technical protections for a local-first design, including server-side credential handling and encryption of saved keys. No software or transmission is perfectly secure. Keep the software updated, bind local dev servers to trusted interfaces, and rotate credentials after a suspected leak.

## 12. Updates

We may update this policy and will change the date on this page. Continued use of the official distribution or website means you understand the updated policy.

## 13. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details published on [vercelai.cn](https://vercelai.cn).
